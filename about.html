Origins of Hot Pot

Hot pot is a beloved communal dining experience with roots reaching back over 2,000 years to ancient China. Beginning as simple bronze cooking vessels during the Shang dynasty, the tradition evolved through the centuries, influenced by nomadic warriors who simmered meats in metal helmets and Yangtze River laborers who created the fiery, numbing mala broth to keep warm. Today, this interactive culinary ritual brings people together around a single simmering pot of flavorful broth, inviting diners to cook a custom selection of thinly sliced meats, fresh vegetables, and handmade noodles right at the table.
