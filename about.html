Our Origin Story

Welcome to The Stemay Wok, where culinary excellence meets a passion for hot pot dining. Our journey began with a vision to create a unique dining experience that celebrates the art of hot pot cooking. Inspired by the rich flavors and communal nature of this beloved cuisine, we set out to craft a menu that showcases the finest ingredients and traditional techniques.
